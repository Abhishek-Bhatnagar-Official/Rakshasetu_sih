# RakshaSetu

### AI-Assisted Disaster Response Orchestration & Decision-Support Platform

> **Bridge towards safety**

RakshaSetu is an AI-assisted disaster-response orchestration platform designed to help authorities convert incoming emergency reports into **prioritized, explainable and resource-aware response recommendations**.

The platform focuses on a practical operational problem:

**When multiple emergencies happen at the same time and response resources are limited, how should available resources be coordinated across competing incidents?**

RakshaSetu processes citizen reports, consolidates related reports, prioritizes incidents, evaluates available resources, calculates geographic travel costs, and uses multi-incident optimization to generate an allocation recommendation.

The final operational decision remains with authorized human authorities.

---

## Overview

During floods, landslides, road blockages, building collapses and other emergencies, authorities may receive multiple reports from different locations within a short period of time.

The challenge is not simply identifying that an emergency exists. The challenge is coordinating a response when:

- Multiple incidents are active simultaneously.
- Several citizen reports may describe the same physical incident.
- Different incidents have different levels of urgency.
- Rescue teams, ambulances, boats and medical teams are limited.
- Resources have different capabilities and locations.
- Travel time and distance affect response effectiveness.
- One resource may be required by multiple incidents.
- Human authorities must retain operational control.

RakshaSetu addresses this through a single operational workflow:

```text
Citizen Reports
      ↓
Understand Reports
      ↓
Consolidate Related Reports
      ↓
Create & Prioritize Incidents
      ↓
Check Resource Availability & Capability
      ↓
Calculate Travel Costs
      ↓
Optimize Across Multiple Incidents
      ↓
Generate Explainable Allocation Plan
      ↓
Human Authority Decision
